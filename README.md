# iowa-organ-privacy
privacy policy for Iowa Organ app
Iowa Organ Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 21, 2026
Iowa Organ is a practice tracking application designed for organ students. The app is developed and maintained by Gregory Hand.
Information Collected
Iowa Organ collects limited information necessary to provide its practice-tracking functionality. This may include:
Student name
User or account identifier
Practice-session information, including practice times, durations, instrument selections, and practice activity
Information necessary to operate and secure the user's account and app sessions
Iowa Organ does not use this information for advertising or marketing and does not sell user information.
How Information Is Used
Information collected by Iowa Organ is used only for app functionality, including:
Identifying the student using the app
Recording and displaying practice sessions
Calculating weekly and semester practice progress
Providing practice history and progress information
Supporting features related to organ-studio practice and instrument use
Maintaining the security and reliable operation of the app
Data Storage and Third-Party Services
Iowa Organ uses Google Firebase services to provide cloud-based application functionality and data storage. Information required by the app may therefore be transmitted to and stored using Google Firebase services.
Iowa Organ does not use student information for third-party advertising and does not share student information with data brokers for tracking or advertising purposes.
Data Retention and Deletion
Practice and account information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's practice-tracking and studio functions.
Users may request deletion of their account information and associated practice data by contacting the developer.
Children
Iowa Organ is intended for authorized organ students and is not designed as a general-purpose application directed toward children.
Changes to This Policy
This privacy policy may be updated if Iowa Organ's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this page.
